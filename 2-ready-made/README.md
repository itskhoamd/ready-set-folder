# 2 · Ready-made workspaces

Same as the starter, but **the folders are already made** for one kind of project.
Use one if you already know what you need, or if your AI is a chat app (claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini) that can't create folders.

| Workspace | For | Download |
|---|---|---|
| 🤖 **[automation-builder](automation-builder/)** | For people building automations and AI agents (n8n, Make, Zapier, small scripts): workflow exports, code, prompts, database changes and setup guides, with secrets kept out. | [⬇ automation-builder.zip](https://github.com/itskhoamd/ready-set-folder/releases/latest/download/automation-builder.zip) |
| 💼 **[client-projects](client-projects/)** | For freelancers and small agencies working for several clients: one folder per client (from a template), a lead pipeline, reusable templates, your own business admin, and client data kept apart. | [⬇ client-projects.zip](https://github.com/itskhoamd/ready-set-folder/releases/latest/download/client-projects.zip) |
| 🎬 **[content-creator](content-creator/)** | For creators making videos, reels or podcasts with AI help: ideas, scripts, raw footage, edits and approved exports, each in its own place, with one episode code tying them together. | [⬇ content-creator.zip](https://github.com/itskhoamd/ready-set-folder/releases/latest/download/content-creator.zip) |
| 🎥 **[film-production](film-production/)** | For production assistants, coordinators and producers on shoots (commercials, short films, events): one folder per job from brief to wrap, blank schedule and petty-cash templates, and a portfolio that grows with every job. | [⬇ film-production.zip](https://github.com/itskhoamd/ready-set-folder/releases/latest/download/film-production.zip) |

## How to use it
1. **Download** a zip, unzip it, rename the folder to your project.
2. **Give it to your AI** and say **"hi"**. It asks a few questions about your project and fills everything in. (Details: `START-HERE.md` inside.)

Not sure which one? Use the **[starter](../1-start-here/)** instead: the AI picks for you.
