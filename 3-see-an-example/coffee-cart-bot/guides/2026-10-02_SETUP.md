# GUIDE: Set up the Bean Cart order bot

> v1, 2026-10-02. **Time:** about 30 min in total. **You need:** your phone with Telegram, the Bean Cart Google Sheet.
> How to use it: do the steps in order. After each step, check the **✅ You should see** line. If it doesn't match, stop and tell the AI which step and what you see instead.

---

## Part A: Create the bot and the sheet tab

### ✅ A0 · Create the Telegram bot (5 min)
1. In Telegram, search **@BotFather** → tap **Start** → send `/newbot`.
2. Name: `Bean Cart Orders` · username: any free name ending in `bot`.
3. BotFather sends a token. **Don't paste it in chat or in any file.** Add it straight into n8n → Credentials → **Telegram API**.

✅ You should see: a credential called "Bean Cart bot" in n8n, and the bot in your Telegram chats.

### ✅ A1 · Add the Orders tab (5 min)
1. Open the Bean Cart sheet → **+** at the bottom → rename the tab `Orders`.
2. Row 1: `Time in` · `Customer` · `Drink` · `Size` · `Sugar` · `Milk` · `Pickup` · `Status`.

✅ You should see: the 8 headers in row 1 of `Orders`.

## Part B: Test with your real menu

### ☐ B0 · Send 5 test orders (10 min)
1. From your own Telegram, send the bot 5 orders the way customers write them.

✅ You should see: 5 new rows in `Orders` within a minute, with the right pickup times.

### ☐ B1 · Try a missing time (2 min)
1. Send `1 cappuccino` with no time.

✅ You should see: the bot replies "What time will you pick it up?"
